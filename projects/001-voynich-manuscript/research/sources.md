# Sources

1. **Voynich manuscript** — Wikipedia
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voynich_manuscript
   Accessed: 2026-07-06
   General encyclopedic overview; well-cited for provenance, dating, and
   Beinecke custody facts, cross-checked against Yale's own page.

2. **Mysterious Voynich manuscript may be a cipher, a new study suggests** —
   Live Science
   https://www.livescience.com/archaeology/mysterious-voynich-manuscript-may-be-a-cipher-a-new-study-suggests
   Accessed: 2026-07-06
   Science-news outlet reporting on recent cipher-hypothesis research; useful
   for current-state-of-decipherment claims.

3. **Keywords and Co-Occurrence Patterns in the Voynich Manuscript: An
   Information-Theoretic Analysis** — PMC / PLOS ONE (peer-reviewed)
   https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3689824/
   Accessed: 2026-07-06
   Peer-reviewed statistical linguistics study; strong primary source for the
   "looks like real language" claim.

4. **The enigma of the Voynich manuscript / Where was the Voynich MS kept and
   how did Voynich find it?** — voynich.nu (Rene Zandbergen)
   https://www.voynich.nu/extra/mondragone.html
   Accessed: 2026-07-06
   Long-running specialist research site widely cited among Voynich
   researchers for detailed provenance; corroborated against Wikipedia and
   academic sources on the Villa Mondragone/Torlonia question.

5. **Voynich MS — Description of the MS / illustrations** — voynich.nu
   https://www.voynich.nu/descr.html
   Accessed: 2026-07-06
   Same specialist site; detailed section-by-section physical description
   used for content/illustration facts.

6. **Radio-carbon dating of the Voynich MS** — voynich.nu
   https://voynich.nu/extra/carbon.html
   Accessed: 2026-07-06
   Summarizes the University of Arizona radiocarbon study; dating figures
   cross-checked against Wikipedia's summary of the same study.
