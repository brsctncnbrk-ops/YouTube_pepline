# Direction Plan — The Voynich Manuscript

## Rhythm & Arc

The video opens slow and ominous (scene_001), then settles into a
deliberate, exploratory pace through the manuscript's physical description
and six illustrated sections (scenes 2-6) — curiosity building steadily,
no rush, letting each strange image breathe. The provenance chapter
(scenes 7-8) stays slow and weighty, shifting from opulent obsession to
hushed secrecy, closing on an eerie, empty beat (the two-century gap).
Energy begins to climb in scene_009 as the codebreaker montage
introduces momentum — this is where the video starts leaning forward.
Scenes 10-12 form the mini-climax: quicker, more analytical, building to
the radiocarbon-dating payoff in scene_012, which resolves the
discoverer-or-forger curiosity loop opened in scene_008. Scene_013
deliberately decelerates — wide, contemplative, unhurried — so the CTA
lands on a calm, reflective note rather than a rushed one. Overall shape:
slow → curious → weighty/eerie → building → climactic reveal → calm close.

Camera motion keywords used below map directly to `factforge-motion`'s
`camera_motion.type`: `zoom_in`, `zoom_out`, `pan_left`, `pan_right`,
`pan_up`, `pan_down`, `static`.

---

### scene_001 (0:00–0:29)

- **Pacing**: Slow, deliberate, ominous open.
- **Emotional flow**: Tension and mystery — draw the viewer in before they
  know what they're looking at.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, subtle-to-moderate intensity — a slow
  push toward the locked glass case, per the storyboard's push-in intent.
- **Close vs. wide**: Starts wide (vault interior) and tightens toward
  medium-close on the case by scene's end.
- **Text animation**: "THE VOYNICH MANUSCRIPT" fades up during the final
  third of the push-in, holds briefly, fades out just before the hard cut
  into scene_002 — don't let it linger into the next scene's cut.
- **Attention direction**: Eye drawn from the dim vault architecture inward
  to the glowing case as the sole warm light source.

### scene_002 (0:29–1:28)

- **Pacing**: Slow, immersive, sensory.
- **Emotional flow**: Intimacy and quiet wonder at the object itself, first
  real contact with the unknown script.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, subtle — a slow tightening glide across
  the ink texture rather than a hard push, matching the macro framing.
- **Close vs. wide**: Extreme close-up throughout.
- **Text animation**: "~240 pages · unknown script · undeciphered for
  100+ years" fades up early, holds through most of the scene, fades out
  ahead of the dissolve into scene_003.
- **Attention direction**: The gloved hand's page-turn pulls the eye
  rightward at the beat's end, priming the dissolve.

### scene_003 (1:28–2:08)

- **Pacing**: Moderate, exploratory — first "section reveal" of the
  montage.
- **Emotional flow**: Strange curiosity; the plants look almost real but
  aren't.
- **Camera movement**: `pan_right`, subtle — slow lateral glide across the
  page spread, consistent with the storyboard's pan-reveal intent.
- **Close vs. wide**: Close-medium on the foreground plant illustration.
- **Text animation**: "126 pages · Herbal section" fades up at scene start,
  fades out at the midpoint to let the illustration hold the frame alone
  before the cut to scene_004.
- **Attention direction**: Pan direction leads the eye from the singular
  plant toward the blurred stack of similar pages, implying scale (100+
  pages of these).

### scene_004 (2:08–2:32)

- **Pacing**: Quicker beat — shortest scene in the montage (24s), keep it
  brisk.
- **Emotional flow**: Intrigue at unexpected order — this is recognizably
  astrology, not gibberish.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, subtle — a slow tightening toward the
  diagram's center that reads as the implied "gentle rotation" from the
  storyboard without literal rotation.
- **Close vs. wide**: Medium-close, diagram centered and dominant.
- **Text animation**: "Astronomical section · zodiac diagrams" fades up
  fast (this scene is short), holds, fades out cleanly before the hard cut
  to scene_005.
- **Attention direction**: Radial diagram naturally pulls the eye to
  center; zoom reinforces it.

### scene_005 (2:32–3:02)

- **Pacing**: Slow, unsettling — the peak strangeness of the section
  montage.
- **Emotional flow**: Discomfort and fascination — "nothing else in
  history looks like this."
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, moderate — a more deliberate push than
  the previous scenes, signaling this is the montage's strangest beat.
- **Close vs. wide**: Close throughout.
- **Text animation**: "Biological section — the 'nymphs'" fades up at
  mid-scene (after the image has had a moment to land unexplained), holds,
  fades before the dissolve to scene_006.
- **Attention direction**: Zoom pulls focus toward the tube-and-vessel
  cluster at center, away from the frame edges.

### scene_006 (3:02–3:36)

- **Pacing**: Moderate, transitional — closing out the six-sections
  montage and pivoting toward the provenance chapter.
- **Emotional flow**: Unresolved curiosity — "recipes for what? Nobody
  knows."
- **Camera movement**: `pan_down`, subtle, settling into a slight `zoom_in`
  on the starred paragraph by scene's end (lead with pan, land on zoom) —
  if the Motion stage needs a single keyword, use `zoom_in` for the final
  and dominant beat.
- **Close vs. wide**: Medium on the jars, tightening to close on the
  starred text.
- **Text animation**: "Pharmaceutical + Recipes sections · purpose
  unknown" fades up with the jars, holds through the pan/zoom, fades out
  before the fade transition to scene_007 — give it a soft fade to match
  the soft transition_out.
- **Attention direction**: Downward pan leads the eye from objects (jars)
  to text (the starred paragraph), setting up the "no answers" beat.

### scene_007 (3:36–5:01)

- **Pacing**: Slow and weighty — the longest early scene (85s), a full
  historical chapter told in one unhurried breath.
- **Emotional flow**: Opulent obsession (Rudolf's study) → solemn
  scholarly effort (Kircher) → eerie emptiness (the two-century gap). Three
  connected beats within one scene.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_out`, subtle — start tighter on the desk and
  letter, slowly widen across the beat to end on the empty shelf, visually
  enacting the widening gap of time. (If finer control is available, the
  desk/letter portion can hold `static` before the `zoom_out` begins.)
- **Close vs. wide**: Medium on the desk/letter opening, wide by the
  empty-shelf close.
- **Text animation**: "c. 1600 → 1666 → two centuries of silence" appears
  in three staggered beats — each date/phrase fades in as its era begins
  in the narration — with the full line held through the final empty-shelf
  moment, then fading before the hard cut to scene_008.
- **Attention direction**: The widening zoom_out mirrors the narration's
  widening time gap, ending attention on the empty gap in the shelf itself.

### scene_008 (5:01–6:21)

- **Pacing**: Moderate, hushed and secretive.
- **Emotional flow**: Suspicion and intrigue — the sale happened in
  secrecy, and the question of Voynich's honesty is first raised here.
- **Camera movement**: `pan_right`, subtle — moving from the library
  shelves and wrapped books toward the dealer's portrait.
- **Close vs. wide**: Wide-to-medium, pushing toward a medium portrait on
  the dealer by scene's end.
- **Text animation**: "1912 · Wilfrid Voynich acquires the manuscript"
  fades up as the pan settles on the dealer, holds, fades out before the
  cut to scene_009.
- **Attention direction**: Pan leads the eye from anonymous stacked books
  to the one specific man responsible for the modern chapter of the story
  — personalizing the mystery right as the "discoverer or forger" question
  is posed.

### scene_009 (6:21–7:49)

- **Pacing**: Energy begins to climb here — this is the pivot from slow
  historical narration into the quicker, building "twist" section.
- **Emotional flow**: Admiration curdling into tension — even the best
  minds in history failed, one after another.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, moderate, on the cryptologist portrait,
  with an implied secondary shift toward the glowing screen element as the
  scene closes (if only one keyword is supported, keep `zoom_in` as the
  dominant, continuing motion across the whole beat).
- **Close vs. wide**: Medium portrait opening, widening slightly to
  include the glowing screen by the end.
- **Text animation**: "WWII codebreakers → modern AI · still unsolved"
  reveals in two parts — "WWII codebreakers" fades in first over the
  period desk, then "→ modern AI · still unsolved" fades in as the screen
  element appears — full line holds briefly then fades before the dissolve
  to scene_010.
- **Attention direction**: Zoom_in plus the screen's glow pulls the eye
  from the historical figure toward the modern data pattern, bridging eras
  within a single shot.

### scene_010 (7:49–8:34)

- **Pacing**: Quicker, analytical — the mini-climax begins in earnest.
- **Emotional flow**: Intellectual excitement — this is the pivot fact of
  the whole video (it behaves like real language).
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, moderate, tightening onto the
  hand-drawn graph lines.
- **Close vs. wide**: Close macro on the diagram.
- **Text animation**: "Statistical tests: it behaves like a real language"
  fades up early and holds for most of the scene — this is the pivot
  statement and deserves the longest on-screen hold in the video — fading
  out only right before the cut to scene_011.
- **Attention direction**: Zoom draws the eye along the graph's rising
  line, visually reinforcing "the data behaves like language."

### scene_011 (8:34–9:04)

- **Pacing**: Brisk — a short, curious aside (30s) within the climax
  sequence.
- **Emotional flow**: Fascinated speculation — "here's how it could have
  been done."
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_in`, subtle, circling attention onto the
  device's engraved surface.
- **Close vs. wide**: Close throughout.
- **Text animation**: "The 'Naibbe' cipher hypothesis" fades up quickly,
  holds, fades out cleanly before the hard cut to scene_012.
- **Attention direction**: Zoom keeps focus on the etched glyphs on the
  device, visually tying the mechanism back to the manuscript's script.

### scene_012 (9:04–9:48)

- **Pacing**: The climactic reveal — the payoff of the scene_008 curiosity
  loop.
- **Emotional flow**: Revelation and quiet awe — Voynich is cleared, and
  the mystery deepens rather than resolves.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_out`, moderate — pulling back from the vellum
  sample to reveal the full closed manuscript, exactly per the storyboard.
- **Close vs. wide**: Starts close on the sample, ends wide on the full
  manuscript.
- **Text animation**: "Radiocarbon dated: 1404–1438" fades up early on the
  close-up sample shot, holds through the entire pull-back so the number
  is still visible when the full manuscript is revealed, then fades before
  the fade transition into scene_013.
- **Attention direction**: The zoom_out's reveal is the whole point of the
  shot — attention should land on the manuscript at the exact moment the
  narration says "he didn't fake the page it's written on."

### scene_013 (9:48–10:56)

- **Pacing**: Slow, settling, contemplative — a deliberate deceleration
  after the climax.
- **Emotional flow**: Quiet reflection opening into an inviting, unhurried
  call to action.
- **Camera movement**: `zoom_out`, subtle, holding wide on the manuscript
  box before the cross-fade to the library exterior.
- **Close vs. wide**: Wide throughout both halves of the scene.
- **Text animation**: "Full manuscript free to view online · What's your
  theory?" fades up in the second half, once the library exterior appears,
  and holds through the end of the video — no fade-out needed since the
  video ends on it.
- **Attention direction**: The slow zoom_out and the cross-fade to the
  library's glowing windows give the eye somewhere calm to rest, closing
  the visual rhythm gently rather than cutting hard.
