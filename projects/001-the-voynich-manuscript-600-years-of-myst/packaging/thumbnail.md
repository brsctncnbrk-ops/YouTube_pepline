# Thumbnail Concepts — The Voynich Manuscript: 600 Years of Mystery

## Concept 1 — "NO ONE CAN READ THIS"
Extreme close-up of the manuscript's open page glowing under candlelight
against the dark teal-shadowed archive (the scene_003/scene_004 macro
handwriting shot).

- **Text overlay:** NO ONE CAN READ THIS
- **Notes:** Bold white/gold condensed type across the upper third, high
  contrast against the dark page. Leave the lower third clear of text so
  the page's flowing script stays the visual hook.

## Concept 2 — "600 YEARS UNSOLVED"
The wide hook shot of the manuscript in its lit glass case at the end of
the dark corridor (scene_001), vignette already dramatic.

- **Text overlay:** 600 YEARS UNSOLVED
- **Notes:** Lower-third text banner, gold accent color matching the
  video's own candlelight palette. Corridor's symmetry keeps the case as
  the clear focal point even at thumbnail size.

Concept 1 (`thumbnail.png`) was produced as an actual image file — a real
frame from the final render with the text overlay composited on top (see
`packaging/thumbnail.png`). Concept 2 is documented here as an alternative
for A/B testing but wasn't rendered to a file.
