# Character Style — The Voynich Manuscript

Three of the 13 scenes feature human figures (scene_002 library scholar,
scene_008 medieval monk, scene_009 WWII-era codebreaker). Observed style:

- **Realism level:** photoreal, detailed period costuming — not stylized or
  illustrated. Faces and hands rendered with realistic proportions and
  skin/fabric texture.
- **Period accuracy:** costuming matches each figure's era — the monk
  (scene_008) in medieval scholar's robes, the codebreaker (scene_009) in
  1940s-appropriate suit at a period-correct desk with cipher-machine props,
  the library figure (scene_002) in a modern-but-timeless tweed/formal
  archivist look.
- **Framing:** figures are always secondary to the manuscript/artifact in
  the frame — shot from the side, in profile, or at a middle distance,
  never a direct-to-camera close-up. This keeps the manuscript itself as
  the visual protagonist across the video.
- **Lighting:** same teal-ambient + warm-candle duality as every other
  scene — no separate "character lighting" treatment.

No recurring named character appears more than once; each human figure is a
one-scene visual device representing a role (archivist, historical scribe,
codebreaker), not a continuing presence.
