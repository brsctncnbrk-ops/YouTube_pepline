# Visual Style Bible — The Voynich Manuscript: 600 Years of Mystery

> **Provenance note:** all 13 scene images already exist (provided directly,
> not generated through `factforge-visual-prompt`). This style bible
> documents the visual language **as observed** across those real images,
> for internal consistency reference — it is retroactive documentation, not
> a forward-looking creative brief.

## Overall visual language

A cinematic, candlelit archival-documentary look — cool teal/cyan ambient
lighting throughout, cut through with warm orange practical light sources
(candles, desk lamps). Photoreal 2.5D-illustration-adjacent rendering:
detailed, moody, slightly desaturated except for the warm light pools.
Every scene reads as "inside a dimly lit rare-books archive at night,"
whether the subject is the manuscript itself, a person, or an object.

## Style consistency token

```
Cinematic archival documentary photography, cool teal ambient lighting
contrasted with warm candlelight, moody atmospheric depth, richly detailed
period-accurate props and parchment, shallow depth of field, high production
value in the style of a prestige history documentary
```

## Consistency check

Verified across all 13 provided images: every one uses the same teal/cyan +
candle-warm lighting duality, the same level of prop/material detail
(aged parchment, wax, wood, brass, glass), and the same photoreal rendering
approach. No scene breaks from this look.
