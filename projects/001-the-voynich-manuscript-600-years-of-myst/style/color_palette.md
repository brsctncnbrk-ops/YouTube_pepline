# Color Palette — The Voynich Manuscript

Observed consistently across all 13 provided images:

- **Ambient/base:** deep teal and cyan-gray (`#1a3a3f`-`#2d4a52` range) —
  the dominant "room tone" of every scene, day or archive-interior.
- **Warm accent (light sources):** candle-flame amber/orange
  (`#f5a623`-`#ffcf7a` range) — every scene has at least one visible warm
  light source (candle, desk lamp, glowing screen) breaking the cool base.
- **Parchment/vellum:** aged cream-tan (`#d8c9a3`-`#e8ddc0` range) for the
  manuscript pages themselves, consistent whether shown close-up or in a
  wide shot.
- **Ink/text:** dark sepia-brown to near-black, never pure black — keeps
  the manuscript's own script legible-but-illegible texture consistent.
- **Museum/exterior (scene_013 only):** the same teal base extended to
  architectural stone and dusk sky, warm light from building windows and
  lamps taking over the "candle" role.

No saturated primary colors appear anywhere — the palette stays desaturated
and moody throughout, consistent with the style token's "cinematic archival
documentary" framing.
